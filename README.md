### About me

I'm a senior frontend architect in Hyderabad, India, with **11+ years** of building web platforms and mobile apps that real people use every day, from truck drivers in container yards to finance teams moving money across borders.

I started in 2014 on native Android and iOS apps for port operations, moved into hybrid mobile with Cordova, Ionic and Capacitor, and now design large Angular platforms. Most recently I designed the microfrontend platform behind a cross-border payments product, where each business domain ships on its own schedule.

- 🏗️ I split big frontends so **every team can ship on its own**, and make them faster while I'm there
- 📱 I build **offline-first mobile apps** that keep working when the signal doesn't
- 🤖 ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot and Claude Code are a natural part of my workflow, and I review every line they produce
- 🌱 Currently going deep on **React and Node.js** on the way to the full **MEAN stack**
- 💼 Open to **client projects, contracts and senior roles** with US, UK and India teams

- **Safety & field-operations app (industrial)**: offline-first Ionic/Capacitor app with SQLite ↔ Firestore sync, gamified modules and on-device face detection
- **SensiWatch mobile (IoT / cold chain)**: real-time shipment monitoring, trip maps, sensor graphs, push alerts and biometric sign-in
- **Accela mobile (gov-tech)**: Ionic 5 + Angular app with ArcGIS JavaScript maps
